First comment:
Hey Product Hunt, Kevin here.

The pattern is always the same. Someone posts "is there an alternative to Calendly that just does round-robin?" The first two or three people who reply with something useful get the click. By the next morning the thread is done.

I kept refreshing searches to catch those, so I built lurk. You paste your URL, it works out who buys and where they post, then watches Reddit and X and tells you, in one sentence, why each post is worth your time. Every post is read by Jev before it reaches you. New leads go to your inbox, Slack or Discord.

A few things I care about:
- It's free. There's no plan to buy.
- It's MIT-licensed. `docker compose up` and there are no limits at all.
- It never posts or drafts replies for you. It finds the conversation; the words are yours.

It's Hypership Day, so tell me what's missing and I'll try to ship it today.

What does it find for your product? I want to hear about the bad leads too.
